About

A bit about how I work

Full stack development with an architect's eye for the bigger picture.

I'm a Full Stack Developer with three years of experience at Blue Reply, building mission-critical digital platforms for the consumer-credit industry. My role has grown from end-to-end feature delivery into owning the architecture itself: surveying and reorganizing hundreds of APIs by domain, modeling systems with C4 diagrams and sequence diagrams, and designing the integrations that connect them to external systems.

I implement what I design end-to-end — in Java (Spring Boot, Quarkus, Apache Camel) on the back end and Angular on the front end — through testing and into CI/CD release. Along the way I've worked hands-on with API management (WSO2, 3scale), performance optimization through caching, event-driven systems with Kafka, and more recently GenAI/OCR solutions for document anti-fraud.

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Catania (110/110 cum laude), and I stay curious about new technologies — GenAI included — always looking for the next interesting technical challenge.
